Privacy notice.

This notice describes the private, single-operator Clip Room tool and this static informational site.

What the tool handles

Clip Room can store source videos, creator names, titles and links, transcripts, sampled frames, editing plans, output videos, quality reports and activity records. The operator supplies footage or accessible source links and records whether the footage is owned or authorized for use. Discovery may retrieve public creator and clip metadata from Twitch.

Google and YouTube

When the channel owner connects Google, Clip Room requests permission to read the YouTube account and upload videos. The tool uses channel information to check that the selected channel is the intended one and uses upload access to send finished videos with their title, description and selected visibility. The operator can choose to disconnect the channel.

Google authorization tokens are stored in protected files on the operator’s machine or server, not in this informational website. You can also review or remove the connection in your Google Account connections. Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Other service providers

When Claude assistance is enabled, the tool may send source and reference video sample frames, transcript excerpts and editing context to Anthropic for analysis and editing decisions. The tool may request public clip or video information and media from Twitch and YouTube. Those providers process requests under their own terms and privacy practices. The operator controls whether to configure these integrations.

Storage and deletion

Footage, exports, transcripts, project records and authorization files are retained on the operator’s configured machine or server. The tool does not automatically purge source media. The operator controls retention, access and deletion. Scheduled SQLite backups retain up to three snapshots per database; they do not include media and are not an off-site backup. Copies maintained separately must also be managed by the operator.

Disconnecting YouTube removes the locally stored connection and attempts to revoke Google authorization. If revocation cannot be confirmed, remove the connection in your Google Account as well.

This site

These static informational pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts and do not request or store Google tokens. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process ordinary server access logs under its own privacy practices.

Contact

For support, visit the Clip Room support page. Issues are public; do not include passwords, tokens or other credentials.
