A room for
the edit.

A private production tool that finds, edits and prepares short videos for the operator’s YouTube channel.

PURPOSE

From source to short.

Clip Room helps its operator work with recordings they own or are authorized to use. It can discover public creator activity, import eligible footage, produce an edit and prepare it for YouTube.

  1. Find and bring in footage

    The tool can monitor public creator activity and accept recordings or accessible video links.

  2. Make the edit

    It transcribes speech, selects a passage, creates captions and checks the rendered result. If configured, Claude may assist with analysis.

  3. Publish to the connected channel

    After the channel owner grants Google permission, the worker can upload finished videos according to the operator’s settings.

ACCESS

For one operator.

Clip Room is a private tool. This site does not offer public accounts, downloads or a sign-up service. Connecting Google is optional and is controlled by the channel owner.

Read how the tool handles information in the privacy notice and review the terms of use.

For support, visit the Clip Room support page. Do not include passwords, tokens or other credentials in a public issue.
